Owner 2026-11-16, on the price report-card mock: "i don't think we want to say things are bad if more expensive, that's not necessarily the case.. and we don't want to say that things are good if more expensive..etc. i think i like the idea of the final score, but we definitely want to be able to back that up".

**Why:** price is not a verdict on a venue; a cheap venue is not "good" and a pricey one is not "bad" (or the reverse).

**How to apply:** in any Tulle pricing UI (Pricing Intelligence, [[project-price-report-redesign-2026-11-15]], Planner): no green-for-cheap / red-or-amber-for-expensive, no award imagery (laurels), no "friendlier", "better", "worse", no letter grades. Describe position only ("above about 43% of comparable venues"). Any headline score must show its working: the venue's figure, what it was compared with (how many spaces, which size band), the market points, what the score leaves out, and sample-size caveats. My interpretation: colour neutrality and the "show the working" panel are how I read the rule, not the owner's words.
