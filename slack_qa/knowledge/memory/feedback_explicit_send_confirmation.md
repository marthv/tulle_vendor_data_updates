2026-10-24: I asked "Go on the past-buyer email? 148 now, 13 on 10-27" in a long multi-item message; owner replied
"past buyer email is good too" and I sent to 148 buyers. Owner: "oh you sent it? i didn't mean for you to send the email..."
Sent mail can't be recalled.

**Why:** customer outreach is irreversible and owner-facing; in a multi-topic message "good" reads as approval of the
idea/draft, not of pressing send.

**How to apply:** for any send to real customers, make it a standalone question with the count and the word send
("Send this to 148 buyers now? yes / no") and only act on an unambiguous yes. Prepare everything up to the send button.
